Our Story

We are a dedicated non-profit organization committed to mitigating the devastating impact of floods on communities and ecosystems.

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect lives, livelihoods, and the environment by advancing sustainable solutions for flood prevention, preparedness, and recovery.

Our Impact

Through partnerships with local communities, governments, and global stakeholders, we build resilience against flood risks. We focus on restoring natural flood defenses, implementing innovative water management strategies, and providing education and resources to empower communities. Together, we aim to create a safer and more sustainable future in the face of increasing flood challenges.

Malaysia is taking proactive steps to address flooding, with initiatives aimed at strengthening flood resilience across the nation.

Over 50% of Malaysian cities and towns have implemented flood mitigation programs, collaborating with local and international partners to reduce flood risks and improve infrastructure.

Thousands of volunteers and community groups in Malaysia support flood recovery efforts, working alongside government agencies to provide aid and rebuild affected areas.
