Fundraising Objectives

The aim of our fundraising campaign is to help those affected by the floods in Malaysia to rebuild their homes and return to normal life. Through donations from the community, we hope to achieve four main objectives: Firstly, to provide building materials and basic amenities to the affected families to help them rebuild their homes as soon as possible. Secondly, to distribute food, water, medicine and other necessities to meet the urgent needs of those affected by the disaster. Thirdly, to support post-disaster education and mental health services to help communities regain confidence, with special attention to the psychological recovery of children and youth. Lastly, we also hope to raise funds for the construction of flood prevention facilities and enhancement of public education on disaster prevention, so as to reduce the impact of flooding on the community in the long run.

Number and Amount of Donations

In our latest fundraising campaign for 2024, we attracted more than 15,000 participants and raised RM2,000,000. This amount of money reflects the love and cohesion of the community and brings tangible help to the people in the affected areas.

Direction of Funds

60%: Support post-disaster reconstruction, including housing repair and community infrastructure reconstruction.
25%: Distribute emergency supplies such as food, drinking water, medicine, etc.
10%: Fund mental health support projects and education recovery programs.
5%: Used for flood warning system optimization and disaster prevention publicity.

History

Over the past five years, our fundraising activities have raised more than RM8,000,000 and helped more than 50,000 affected residents. These funds have been used to rehabilitate homes, provide emergency supplies, and restore educational and psychological support services. These efforts not only provide immediate assistance to those affected by the disaster, but also lay the foundation for the long-term development of the community. Through your generosity, we hope to continue to bring warmth, support and hope to those affected by the disaster!


Our Events

🌟 Event: Hunger30 – Fundraising for Flood Victims

Event Description
Hunger30 is a 30-hour fasting event designed to raise funds and awareness for communities impacted by floods. The event features educational talks, team-building activities, and real-life stories to help participants understand the profound impact of disasters.

Fundraising Results
Latest Event (2024): Raised 1,200,000 MYR
Participants: Over 8,000 people
Fund Allocation: Used to distribute food packages, medical supplies, and aid reconstruction efforts.

Past Records
2023: Raised 950,000 MYR for emergency supplies for 20,000 flood victims
2022: Collected 850,000 MYR to assist in clearing and repairing five heavily affected communities
2021: Raised 1,100,000 MYR to fund psychological support programs for 3,500 victims

🌊 Event: Flood Relief Walkathon



Event Description
This walkathon raises awareness of climate change and flood issues through a healthy activity. Registration fees and individual fundraising contribute to flood emergency funds. The event route spans Kuala Lumpur’s historic landmarks, symbolizing unity and hope.


Fundraising Results
Latest Event (2024): Raised 750,000 MYR
Participants: Over 5,000 walkers
Fund Allocation: Expanded shelter facilities and provided water purification equipment.

Past Records
2023: Raised 600,000 MYR to build three new emergency shelters
2022: Collected 520,000 MYR to distribute water purification systems to 12 villages
2021: Raised 700,000 MYR for post-disaster infrastructure repair
